**描述**
```
你有多久没从屏幕上抬起眼了？

护眼管家会安静地待在后头，用前置摄像头在本机判断你是否还坐在屏幕前。只要连续注视超过你设定的时长，它就会拉起一块全屏的实拍绿植画面，配上一句轻声提醒，让你真的离开屏幕看一会儿远处。

【它怎么工作】
1. 前置摄像头每秒做一次本地人脸检测，只回答「有没有人」，画面不保存、不上传。
2. 连续注视达到设定时长（默认 30 分钟）就触发休息屏。
3. 你起身离开，计时会重新开始——它不会冤枉你。
4. 休息倒计时结束，自动回到原来的界面。

【主要功能】
• 全屏休息页：实拍绿植视频 + 倒计时 + 语音提醒
• 本地人脸检测：全部在本机完成，无网络请求、无画面留存
• 主题色自定义：10 套预设配色 + 自由取色
• 中英双语：一键切换
• 可调时长：1–60 分钟注视阈值，5–60 秒休息时长

【关于 iOS 的一点说明】
iOS 不允许第三方 App 在后台长期占用摄像头，也不允许窗口盖住别的 App。因此在本应用处于前台时，摄像头检测与全屏休息页完全可用；当你切换到其它 App 时，会改为发送本地通知提醒你休息。若需要更强的强制力，可在「屏幕使用时间」里为常用 App 设置限额。

【隐私】
摄像头画面只在本机内存中处理，检测完立即丢弃。不收集任何个人信息，不接入第三方统计或广告 SDK。

【适合谁】
写代码的、赶稿的、刷题的——所有一坐下就忘了时间的人。
```

## 二、English


**Subtitle (30)**


iPad subtitle: `Big-screen eye care, forced breaks`

**Promotional text (170)**
```
The front camera tells, on device, whether you are still at the screen. After 30 minutes of viewing it takes over with greenery footage and a spoken reminder. 10 themes, bilingual, no network, no data collected.
```

**Description**
```
How long has it been since you looked away from the screen?

EyeGuard sits quietly in the background and uses the front camera — entirely on your device — to tell whether you are still there. Once you have been looking at the screen longer than your chosen interval, it takes over with real footage of greenery and a gentle spoken reminder, so you actually look away for a moment.

HOW IT WORKS
1. The front camera runs a local face check about once a second. It only answers "is someone there" — frames are never saved or uploaded.
2. Continuous viewing for your interval (30 minutes by default) triggers the break screen.
3. Stand up and walk away and the timer resets — it will not blame you.
4. When the countdown ends, you are back to what you were doing.

FEATURES
• Full-screen break: greenery video, countdown and a natural voice
• On-device face detection — no network calls, no frames kept
• Custom theme colour: 10 presets plus a free colour picker
• Bilingual, with adjustable viewing interval (1–60 min) and break length (5–60 s)

A NOTE ABOUT iOS
iOS does not allow third-party apps to keep the camera running in the background, nor to draw over other apps. So while EyeGuard is in the foreground the camera check and the full-screen break work fully; when you switch to another app it falls back to a local notification. For stronger enforcement, use Screen Time limits on the apps that distract you most.

PRIVACY
Camera frames are processed in memory and discarded immediately. No personal data is collected, no analytics or ad SDKs are bundled.

WHO IT IS FOR
Developers, writers, students — anyone who sits down and forgets the clock.
```
